HOW TO PLAY

1. Starting the Game
One player clicks Create Game to generate a 4-digit room code.
The second player clicks Join Game and enters the code.
Both players must click "I'M READY" before the match starts.

2. Controls
• Player 1 (Left - Red paddle): W = Up, S = Down
• Player 2 (Right - Blue paddle): ↑ Arrow = Up, ↓ Arrow = Down
• On mobile/touch: Drag your finger up or down on your side of the screen.

3. How the Ball Works
The faster you hit the ball near the edge of your paddle, the more angle and speed it gets.
The ball bounces off the top and bottom edges. If the ball goes past your paddle, the opponent scores a point.

4. Scoring & Ready System
First player to reach 5 points wins the match.
After every point scored, both players must click "I'M READY" again to resume play.

5. Winning the Match
The first to 5 points wins!
You can immediately start a new match or a rematch by clicking Play Again, but each new game the ball and paddles will become faster.
