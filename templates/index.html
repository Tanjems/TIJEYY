HOW TO PLAY

1. Start the Game
Create a room or join with a 4-digit code. Both players must click "I'M READY" to begin.

2. Controls
• Player 1 (Left): W = Up, S = Down
• Player 2 (Right): ↑ Arrow = Up, ↓ Arrow = Down
• Mobile: Drag finger up/down on the screen

3. Scoring
First player to reach 5 points wins.
After every point, both players must click "I'M READY" again.

4. Pause
Click the ⏸ PAUSE button anytime.

5. Win Condition
The first to 5 points wins the match.
