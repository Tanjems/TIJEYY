HOW TO PLAY

  1. Starting the Game
    One player clicks Create Game to get a 4-digit room code.
    The other player clicks Join Game and enters that code.
    Both players must click "I'M READY" to start the match.

  2. Controls
    • Player 1 (Left - Red paddle): W = Up, S = Down
    • Player 2 (Right - Blue paddle): ↑ Arrow = Up, ↓ Arrow = Down
    • On mobile: Drag your finger up/down on your side of the screen.

  3. How the Ball Works
    Hit the ball with your paddle. The spot where the ball hits your paddle changes its speed and angle.
    The ball bounces off the top and bottom walls. If it passes your paddle, the opponent scores.

  4. Scoring
    First player to reach 5 points wins the match.
    After every point, both players must click "I'M READY" again to continue.

  5. Winning the Match
    The first player to 5 points wins!
    You can play again immediately by clicking Play Again.
